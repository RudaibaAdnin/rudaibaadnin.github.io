Rudaiba Adnin

CS Ph.D. Candidate, Northeastern University

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student at Northeastern University, Khoury College of Computer Sciences. I work in the Technology, Equity, and Accessibility (TEA) Lab. My advisor is Prof. Maitraye Das.

My research focuses on the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), Computer-Supported Cooperative Work (CSCW), and Accessible Computing.

In my PhD research, I study the accessibility of GenAI tools from the perspectives of blind and low-vision individuals. Alongside, I design and develop accessible learning technologies to support and enhance accessible GenAI interaction.
